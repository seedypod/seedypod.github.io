# seedypod user guide

seedypod core is the part you interact with. It serves the web UI at `http://seedypod.local:3000/`, runs your light curves and watering schedules, tracks the grow cycle and its reminders, records sensor history and camera stills, and raises alerts. It never touches a pin itself: every command goes to `hald`, the hardware daemon, which applies the safety rules before anything moves.

<Callout type="warning">
  Nothing in this guide has run on a real Gardyn yet. The screenshots are the real web UI running
  against the simulated tower. Hardware behaviour, pin assignments and sensor addresses are
  documented assumptions until the [Phase 1 checklist](/developers/hardware/#phase-1-checklist) is
  done.
</Callout>

## What you can do

- **Grow light**: turn it on or off, set brightness from 0 to 100 %, and draw daily light curves with sunrise and sunset ramps.
- **Water**: run the pump for a bounded time, watch the tank level and pump power, and schedule watering cycles.
- **Grow cycle**: start a cycle, move it through germination, seedling, vegetative and harvest, and get thinning, root-check, harvest and nutrient reminders.
- **Pods**: track what is planted in each of the 30 slots and flag pods that need attention.
- **Cameras**: take stills from the upper and lower cameras and scrub through a timelapse.
- **Alerts**: see low water, pump faults, board over-temperature and reminders, and acknowledge them.
- **Integrations**: Home Assistant over MQTT, AI agents over MCP, and anything else over REST.
- **Backups**: download one any time, or let it go to your bucket every night. A dead SD card is a reflash and **Restore from a backup**.

## Safety you can count on

The pump is the one part that can damage the tower: running dry burns it out, and the board browns out above about 30 % duty. `hald` puts every pump command through a single guard, whether it comes from the web UI, a schedule, Home Assistant, an agent or the physical button:

- every run has a deadline, 900 seconds at most by default;
- duty is capped at 30 %;
- the pump will not start when the tank reads low, or when the water sensor is unavailable;
- the pump will not start, and stops, when the board is too hot;
- a stop caused by a dry tank or heat **latches** the pump off until you acknowledge it.

[ADR 0009](/developers/adr/0009-pump-safety-guard/) has the full rules.
