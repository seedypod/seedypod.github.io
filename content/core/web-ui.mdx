# Web UI tour

Open `http://seedypod.local` from any browser on your network (`:3000` works too; see [Install](/core/installation/#open-the-app)). Changes made anywhere, including from Home Assistant, the Home app, an agent or a schedule, appear immediately: the UI listens to core's live event stream instead of polling.

The sidebar groups thirteen screens: **Garden** (Home, Grow, Plan, Care), **Control** (Light, Water, Autopilot), **Observe** (Cameras, Analysis) and **System** (Status, Sensors), with **Alerts** and **Settings** at the foot. Above them, **Search** (⌘K) finds any screen, setting or docs page, and a summary keeps the light, tank and air readings and a **Needs you** list in view. On a phone a tab bar holds Home, Grow, Light, Water and Settings.

## Welcome

A fresh install opens on **Welcome** instead of Home, with two choices. **Set up a new garden** starts from the defaults. **Restore from a backup** brings a unit back from a downloaded file or from the backups in your bucket ([Replace a dead SD card](/core/installation/#replace-a-dead-sd-card)).

## Garden

### Home

Your tower in 3D: every pod colored by its status, the light bars at their level and the tank's water level. Drag to turn it, ⌘-scroll to zoom, and click a pod to open it. The day of the grow and a count of growing, ready, attention and empty pods sit above it. **Light zones** colors the pods by how much light each gets.

### Grow

The current grow cycle and its stage, the [grow protocol](/core/grow-cycle/#grow-protocol) card with the stage's watering, light, feed and checks, and all 30 pods in three towers, each with its plant and leaf cover. Click a pod for its card: status, a photo crop with a slider through its history, the leaf-cover trend, and the plant, status and notes to edit. **Tower** switches to the 3D view. See [Grow cycle and pods](/core/grow-cycle/).

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-grow.png"
  alt="Grow screen on day 19 of the vegetative stage, with the Fruiting protocol card in Maintenance and its watering, light, feed and checks"
/>

### Plan

Plan the next planting. Pick plants from Gardyn's catalog (or **Add plant** for one it lacks) and the planner places them by light zone, around the pods already planted: high light in the centre, medium around it, low at the top and bottom. Click a planned pod for why it sits there. **3D** and **2D** switch views, and **Home · 30** and **Studio · 16** switch models. Below are a staggered sowing schedule, so harvests don't all land at once, and your saved plans: **Open** one to see it again, **Apply** to write it onto the pods. An agent can do the same over MCP, starting from a pasted seed order ([Plan from an order](/core/mcp/#plan-from-an-order)).

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-plan.webp"
  alt="Plan screen with the 3D tower colored by light zone, and the plant catalog with counts"
/>

### Care

What the tower needs, worked out on the unit with no AI model. It lists what a planted unit is missing (no light or pump schedule on, a tank sensor absent or suspect), then the chores (thin, trim roots, top off, refresh, feed, clean), each due, soon or fine, from a reading the unit trusts or a calendar guess that says so. Below are the tank log you record chores in, starter [Autopilot](/core/autopilot/) rules, and how to put an assistant on top over [MCP](/core/mcp/). Home shows what's due now.

## Control

### Light

The strip at the top says what the light is doing and why, with a stepped slider: **Auto · Off · 25 · 50 · 75 · 100**. **Auto** follows the sun, with hours from the grow protocol; any other stop holds the light, by default until the curve's next change, and the hold bar can make it an hour or until you resume ([The Light strip](/core/schedules/#the-light-strip)). The chart shows today's curve and where it is now, with the LED hours against their rated life. The **Today** timeline marks daylight from sunrise to sunset and how far the light extends past it. Below, you create and edit [light curves](/core/schedules/#light-curves), Auto's first.

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-light.png"
  alt="Light screen at 85 % following the daylight curve: the strip with its Auto, Off, 25, 50, 75 and 100 stops, today's chart, and the Today timeline"
/>

### Water

The pump strip says whether the pump is running and when it next runs, with a run-once control: pick 1, 5, 10 or 15 minutes and press **Run**. Below it, the tank level as a percentage and an estimated volume, over 24 hours, 7 or 30 days, down to the cutoff at which a low tank refuses to start. Then the **Today** timeline, the [grow protocol](/core/grow-cycle/#grow-protocol) card, [Your schedules](/core/schedules/#your-schedules) with the schedule editor, and the recent scheduled runs.

If the pump is latched off after a fault, the header says what tripped it (`pump fault: dry-run`), and **Run** is disabled until you acknowledge the fault on the Alerts screen.

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-water.png"
  alt="Water screen: the pump strip with its run-once control, the week's tank level at 87 %, the Today timeline, and the Fruiting protocol card"
/>

### Autopilot

Rules that act on readings rather than the clock: notify you, set the light, run the pump or a doser, or flag a pod when a condition holds. Each rule reads back as a sentence, starts in dry run, and shows a 7-day backtest before you arm it. A timeline of the last 24 hours and an activity log say what each rule did or would have done, and the switch at the top turns them all off. See [Autopilot](/core/autopilot/).

## Observe

### Cameras

The latest still from the upper and lower cameras, each with **Capture now** (or **Capture both**), and a strip of the last two days' frames: drag to scrub, or press **Play** for a timelapse. Frames you've played are kept in the browser, so a replay doesn't download them again. Stills are taken automatically at the interval set in **Settings → Cameras** (60 minutes by default; 0 turns it off).

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-cameras.png"
  alt="Cameras screen with the upper and lower camera stills and their timelapse strips"
/>

### Analysis

How the camera analysers score each pod's leaf cover. One card shows the latest photos from both cameras, or one, with each analyser's result and **Pod bounds** to see or edit the box each pod is scored on. The analyser cards say how each one works, which is **primary** for growth trends, and **Re-run** to rescore the stored photos. A table of all 30 pods compares the analysers side by side, and **Export dataset** downloads the photos and scores. See [Capture analysis](/core/analysis/).

## System

### Status

The whole unit at a glance, live: a health ring with the checks that pass, core, hardware daemon, Wi-Fi, Home Assistant, SD card and uptime, any active alert, a timeline of today's light, water and air with the next scheduled job, and cards for the light, water and climate.

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-status.png"

### Sensors

Every sensor the unit has, its part and address, whether it's ready, and its latest reading with a chart over 6 hours, 24 hours or 7 days. **Add sensor** adds a pH, EC or water-temperature probe with a guided calibration. See [Adding a probe](/core/sensors/).

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-sensors.png"
  alt="Sensors screen with environment, water and pump readings and their charts, and the device list"
/>

### Alerts

Active alerts, or the full history, and plants flagged from Grow. Each alert has **Acknowledge** (or **Done** for a reminder) and **Dismiss**.

| Alert                                                      | Raised when                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Water low                                                  | The tank reading crosses the low-water threshold.                                            |
| Pump fault                                                 | The pump stopped because the tank ran dry, it drew too little current, or the board got hot. |
| Board over temperature                                     | The board temperature reached the cutoff (36 °C by default).                                 |
| Camera missing                                             | A camera failed to capture.                                                                  |
| Hardware daemon offline                                    | Core lost contact with `hald`.                                                               |
| Thin seedlings, Check roots, Harvest window, Add nutrients | A grow reminder is due.                                                                      |
| Off-device copy failed                                     | Photos couldn't be copied to your mirror bucket.                                             |
| _Rule name_: _message_                                     | An Autopilot rule's notify action; clears when the rule's condition stops holding.           |

<Callout type="warning">
  Acknowledging a pump fault re-enables the pump. Fix the cause first: refill the tank, or let the
  board cool.
</Callout>

### Settings

Sections down the side: unit name, timezone, the unit's location for [Auto light](/core/schedules/#auto-light) (empty uses the timezone's city; **Use this device's location** asks the browser, on `https` only) and units; tank calibration (distance when full and when empty, and the tank capacity); grow reminder days; the camera capture interval; the [Home Assistant bridge](/core/home-assistant/#core-bridge); an optional [API key](/core/privacy/#who-can-reach-it); **Apple Home** ([HomeKit](/core/homekit/)); [off-device storage and analysis](/core/analysis/#mirror-photos-off-the-unit-opt-in) (opt-in); **Storage** and **SD card**, the card's writes per day and a **Database** view of what's writing, table by table; **Backup**, which downloads a backup, shows the nightly bucket backups, restores one and undoes a restore ([Maintenance → Backups](/core/maintenance/#backups)); a link to **Wi-Fi**, where you change networks or open the setup hotspot ([Change Wi-Fi](/core/installation/#change-wi-fi)); **Updates**, which checks for and installs a new release ([Maintenance → Updates](/core/maintenance/#updates)); and **About**, with the core and daemon versions and the unit id.

`http://seedypod.local/kiosk` is a wall-display mode: big numbers for the tank, air temperature, humidity, light and pump, plus any active alerts, with no sidebar. It refreshes on its own every few seconds.
