# Knowledge

These notes are where seedypod's numbers come from. When the web UI suggests a watering
cadence, a dose or a pH or EC target, it links the section here it is taken from. Each note
lists its sources; most are agent-compiled drafts that no human has reviewed yet.

- [Nutrients](/knowledge/nutrients/) — Feeding doses, pH and EC targets, from Gardyn and from owners.
- [Watering](/knowledge/watering/) — Pump-cycle starting points by growth stage, and how to judge them by the roots.
- [Plants](/knowledge/plants/) — The light-zone grid, measured, and where the plant catalog's fields come from.

Hardware facts, safety limits and evaluations are developer evidence and stay in core's [knowledge bundle](https://github.com/seedypod/core/blob/ccb03c6ad1e4328ba416e6f96dd177b7bb9a96bf/knowledge/index.md).
