# Watering schedules

<Callout type="warning">
  Draft. An agent compiled this note from the sources below; no human review is claimed.
</Callout>

Gardyn doesn't publish a watering cadence: its Smart Watering adjusts the pump to the plants. The cadences below are what owners on r/Gardyn report running, gathered as starting points, not Gardyn's guidance; adjust them by the roots. Times are the unit's local time.

## Seedlings

Week 1. Germination mode runs a 15 min cycle on day 1, then another around day 7; after that soak, water once a day: 10 min (5–15 min) at 08:00. Gardyn waits a few days to a week after sowing before the second watering.

## Young plants

Weeks 2–3. Twice a day, 6 min (5–8 min), at 08:00 and 16:00. Start at once a day for 5 min and move to two or three times a day as the roots grow.

## Mature greens and herbs

Three weeks on. Three times a day, 6 min (5–8 min), at 08:00, 14:00 and 20:00.

## Large or fruiting plants

Tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers. Five times a day, 5 min, every 4 hours while the lights are on: 06:00, 10:00, 14:00, 18:00 and 22:00. With a shorter light day, keep only the runs inside it.

## Judging a schedule

- Check the roots. Dry, brittle roots want another cycle or a longer one; roots sitting in pooled water just before the next cycle want less.
- Space the cycles across the light hours, so pods don't dry out under the LEDs.
- Many growers switch Gardyn's smart watering off after germination, for a schedule they can predict.
