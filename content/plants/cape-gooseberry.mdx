# Cape Gooseberry

_Physalis peruviana_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–28 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 80 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Each fruit grows inside a papery husk, and the husk usually dries out and fades as the fruit ripens. Ripe fruit is yellow-orange all over and drops on its own, or you can shake the branches lightly to release it. Confirm the fruit is fully yellow before eating, and leave fallen green fruit on a windowsill to finish ripening. Never eat green fruit, because unripe fruit is poisonous.

## Pruning

Regular pruning is needed. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown. Trim back branches that grow out of the light.

## Support

Tie the plant up as it matures so the fruit load doesn't pull it over.

## Pollinating

Hand-pollination is needed. Once flowers open, shake the whole plant to mimic wind, or brush inside each bloom with a fingertip or small brush.

## Pests and disease

Watch for thrips.
