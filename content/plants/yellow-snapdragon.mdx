# Yellow Snapdragon

_Antirrhinum majus_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–20 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 80–115 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

For a vase, cut stems at a 45° angle just above a leaf node, at whatever height you want. Leave ⅓ of the plant each time so it keeps growing and blooms in waves.

## Pruning

Deadhead spent blooms to push new buds.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.
