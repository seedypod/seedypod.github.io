# Arugula

_Eruca sativa_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Don’t thin. Let every sprout in the pod grow.
- **Sprouts in:** 4–21 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 30–40 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert
- **Yield:** Light

## Harvest

It stays least bitter when cut all at once: when outer leaves are 2-3 inches tall, cut every leaf at its base. For repeat picking, take outer leaves at 2-3 inches and remove no more than 1/3 of the plant at a time. Bigger leaves taste hotter and more bitter.

## Pruning

Cut yellow flower stems as they form to hold off bolting.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.

## Climate

Heat makes it bolt and turn bitter.
