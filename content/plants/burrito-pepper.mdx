# Burrito Pepper

_Capsicum annuum_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–28 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 80–95 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Pick at any color once a pepper is firm and about 4 inches long. Cut each pepper off at its own stem.

## Pruning

Little pruning is needed. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown. Trim back branches that grow out of the light.

## Support

Tie the plant up as it and its fruit mature.

## Pollinating

Flowers show up 4-6 weeks after sprouting. Pinch off that first round of buds so the plant puts energy into roots and leaves. Pollinate the next flowers by shaking the whole plant or brushing inside each bloom with a fingertip or small brush.

## Pests and disease

Mild edema is a harmless disorder and won't stop this plant from producing heavily. Watch for aphids.
