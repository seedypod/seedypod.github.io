# Red Cherry Tomato

_Lycopersicum esculentum_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–28 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 60 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

Pick when fruit is red all the way to the stem and gives slightly when pressed. Ripe tomatoes left on the plant too long can split. Twist each one until it snaps free, or cut its stem with shears or a knife.

## Pruning

This dwarf tomato needs very little pruning. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown.

## Pollinating

Flowers show up 4-6 weeks after sprouting. Pinch off that first round of buds just behind the base of each flower head so the plant puts energy into roots and leaves. Pollinate later flowers by shaking the whole plant or swirling a fingertip or small brush inside each bloom.
