# Sweet Thai Basil

_Ocimum basilicum var. thyrsiflora_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–10 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 64 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Frequent harvests extend its life. Take single leaves and flower buds, or cut the stem 1/4 to 1/2 inch above the nodes, the small leaf pair or knobs just above a stem's two largest leaves. Leave 4-6 inches of plant so it keeps growing.

## Pruning

Pick off brown-spotted leaves. For bushier growth, cut stems above the nodes regularly and pinch off flower buds as they appear.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids and spider mites.
