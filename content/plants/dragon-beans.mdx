# Dragon Beans

_Phaseolus vulgaris_ · Legume

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 55–70 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–90 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert
- **Yield:** Light

## Harvest

Pods are ready when flat, about 6 inches long, and pale yellow streaked with purple, usually 1-2 weeks after flowering. Snip each pod off at its own stem. Picking often prompts the plant to set more pods.

## Pruning

Little pruning is needed. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown.

## Support

The vine needs support as it grows. Train it up the tower columns with a trellis and plant ties to carry the pods.

## Pollinating

White, pink, and purple flowers appear 4-6 weeks after sprouting. Help them set pods by shaking the plant gently or swirling a fingertip or small brush inside each bloom.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids and thrips.
