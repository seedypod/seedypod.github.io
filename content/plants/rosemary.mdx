# Rosemary

_Salvia rosmarinus (Rosmarinus officinalis)_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 15–25 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 42 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Light

## Harvest

It grows slowly, so wait until it reaches 8 inches tall before harvesting. Cut off the top half of one or more branches, then strip the leaves from the stem if you like.

## Pruning

Pick off any leaf that develops brown spots.

## Pests and disease

Watch for spider mites.
