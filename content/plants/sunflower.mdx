# Sunflower

_Helianthus annuus_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 65–75 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Light

## Harvest

For petals, snip each flower at its stem and pull the petals off the head. For a vase, cut just before the bloom opens, when the petals are nearly unfurled, and stand it in water.

## Pruning

Deadhead fading blooms at their own stems to prompt new buds.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.
