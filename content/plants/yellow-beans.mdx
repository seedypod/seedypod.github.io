# Yellow Beans

_Phaseolus vulgaris_ · Legume

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 68 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once the roots are 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner

## Harvest

Pods start green and turn yellow as they ripen. Pick round, fleshy pods 5 inches long and about pencil-thick, while still tender and before full maturity, usually 1-2 weeks after flowering. Snip each pod at its stem. The plant sets most of its crop at once, but regular picking keeps it producing.

## Pruning

Little pruning is needed. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown.

## Support

The plant stays compact, but a trellis and plant ties up the tower columns help carry the pods.

## Pollinating

Flowers appear 4-6 weeks after sprouting. Help them set pods by shaking the plant gently to mimic wind, or swirling a fingertip or small brush inside each bloom.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids and spider mites.
