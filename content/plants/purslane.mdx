# Purslane

_Portulaca oleracea_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–21 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 50–70 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner

## Harvest

Frequent picking extends the plant's life and keeps stems from getting leggy. Pinch single leaves, or cut stems just above a growth node with clean shears. Leave ⅓ of the plant's height each time so it keeps growing.

## Pruning

Pull off leaves that develop brown spots.
