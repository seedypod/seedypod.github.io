# Viola

_Viola cornuta_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 50 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 65–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner

## Harvest

Cut flowers at the stem once fully open.

## Pruning

Pinch fading flowers off below the head to make way for new blooms.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.
