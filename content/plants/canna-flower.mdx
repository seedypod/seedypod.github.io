# Canna Flower

_Canna generalis_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 75–85 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once the roots are 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

Expect several waves of blooms from the main stem.

## Pruning

Deadhead fading blooms at their own stems to prompt new buds.

## Support

Grow it in a bottom moderate-light spot so its height stays within the light. Steady the tall stalks with a plant belt as it matures.

## Climate

In warmer months it can move outdoors, where it may reach 4 feet. It returns each year in warm climates.
