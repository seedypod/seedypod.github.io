# Cilantro

_Coriandrum sativum_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–21 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 50–55 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Light

## Harvest

At 6 inches tall, start cutting the larger outer stalks at their base, or take single leaves or stems cut above the nodes. Frequent harvests extend its life. Keep 1/3 of the plant in place if you want it to keep producing.

## Pruning

Pick off any leaf that develops brown spots.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.

## Climate

Heat makes it bolt and turn bitter.
