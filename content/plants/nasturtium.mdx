# Nasturtium

_Tropaeolum majus_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–18 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 55–65 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

Cut flowers at the stem once fully open. Leaves can be picked any time, but wait until the plant is 4-6 inches long with healthy roots so it keeps producing. Past that size, frequent harvesting helps it.

## Pruning

The root system is large, so check and trim roots twice a month. Pinch fading flowers off below the head to make way for new blooms. Older foliage browns as new growth comes in, and frequent picking of flowers and leaves keeps it healthy and productive.

## Support

It trails rather than climbs, so it needs no upward support. Place it on an outer column in a moderate or minimum light zone where it has room to hang. To grow it upward instead, guide the vine along a trellis with plant belts.
