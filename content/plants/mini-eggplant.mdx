# Mini Eggplant

_Solanum melongena_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–10 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 50–85 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Pick once fruit is 2-3 inches long with glossy skin, cutting at each stem with clean shears. Dull skin means the fruit is overripe. Picking often keeps new flowers and fruit coming.

## Pruning

Regular pruning is needed. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown, and trim back branches that grow out of the light. After flowering starts, remove the suckers that sprout where leaves and side branches meet the main stalk. This sends more energy to the fruit.

## Support

Tie the plant up as it matures so the fruit load doesn't pull it over.

## Pollinating

Hand-pollination is needed. Once purple flowers open, shake the whole plant or brush inside each bloom with a fingertip or small brush.

## Pests and disease

Mild edema is a harmless disorder and won't stop this plant from producing heavily. Watch for aphids.
