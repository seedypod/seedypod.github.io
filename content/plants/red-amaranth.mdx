# Red Amaranth

_Amaranthus cruentus_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–10 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 100–120 days
- **Height:** under 2 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Young leaves are good raw, while older leaves are better cooked like spinach. Keep at least 1/3 of the plant for regrowth. For grain, wait until the flowers turn brown on the plant.

## Pruning

Trim off yellow or brown leaves when they show up.

## Support

Use a plant belt to hold it up as it gets taller.

## Pests and disease

Watch for thrips. It needs good airflow, so keep neighboring slots capped and empty and harvest often.
