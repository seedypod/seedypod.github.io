# Red Mustard

_Brassica juncea_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–21 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 21–45 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

Young leaves have better flavor and texture than older ones. Pick outer leaves at 2-3 inches just above the base, keeping at least 1/3 of the plant for regrowth. For a single harvest, let the head mature and cut it at the base.

## Pruning

Cut yellow flower stems as they form to hold off bolting.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.

## Climate

Heat makes it bolt and turn bitter.
