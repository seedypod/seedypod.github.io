# Lemongrass

_Cymbopogon citratus_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 100 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once the roots are 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

For cooking, choose stalks at least 1/4 inch thick and cut or snap them off near the yCube, since the base is the most tender and fragrant part. Peel away the foliage and woody layers to reach the soft core. Leaves can be snipped from the outside of the plant at any time and hung to dry.

## Pruning

Older outer leaves may brown as the plant shifts energy to new growth. Trim those brown leaves away.

## Pests and disease

Watch for spider mites.
