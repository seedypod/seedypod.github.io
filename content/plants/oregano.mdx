# Oregano

_Origanum heracleoticum_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 80–90 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Light

## Harvest

Once it is at least 8 inches tall, harvest often to extend its life. Take single leaves or cut stems above the nodes. Leave 1/3 of the plant so it keeps growing.

## Pruning

Pick off any leaf that develops brown spots.

## Pests and disease

Watch for spider mites.
