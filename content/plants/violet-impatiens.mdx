# Violet Impatiens

_Impatiens walleriana_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–10 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 60–75 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 65–75 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

The flowers are edible. Cut the stem at a 45° angle just below the bloom and use whole flowers or loose petals.

## Pruning

Shorten stems by ⅔ when you want a smaller plant. Deadhead spent blooms to push new buds, and snip off yellow or brown leaves. Always keep at least a third of the plant so it can rebloom.

## Pests and disease

Red blotches under the leaves are normal for this variety. Pests rarely trouble it.

## Climate

Long stretches of heat stall its growth.
