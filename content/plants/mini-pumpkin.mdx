# Mini Pumpkin

_Cucurbita pepo_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–14 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 100–130 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Pick when a pumpkin is about 2-3 inches across, 1-2 inches tall, and fully bright orange. Cut it from the vine with clean scissors, leaving 1-2 inches of stem attached. Expect 2-3 pumpkins per plant, depending on care.

## Pruning

Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown. Top the main stem once it passes 2 feet so side branches fill in and the vine stays inside the light. As flowers fade, pinch off any without a fruit forming, just below the flower head, so the plant puts energy into its next flush of blooms. Some leaf stress while fruit ripens is normal.

## Support

Once the plant is 6 inches tall, settle it in its permanent spot so you can train the vine up the center column. Use a trellis and plant ties to guide the vine and hold the heavy fruit.

## Pollinating

The plant grows separate male and female flowers, and fruit needs both. Male flowers open first on thin, straight stems, while female flowers open later on a round, swollen base like a tiny fruit. Move pollen between them with a fingertip or small brush, and note that a stressed plant drops its female flowers almost right away. Growing 2 plants at once makes timing easier, and a male flower stripped of petals keeps 1-2 days in a dry, airtight container in the fridge.

## Pests and disease

This plant is a heavy feeder. Use the bloom-and-fruit nutrient dose of 1 teaspoon per gallon of tank water. Its roots grow vigorously, so check and trim them twice a month. Grow no more than 2 Mini Pumpkin yCubes on the tower at once, and watch for aphids and spider mites.
