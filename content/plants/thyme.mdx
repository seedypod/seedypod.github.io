# Thyme

_Thymus vulgaris_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–21 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 90–95 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

Frequent harvests extend its life and keep it rounder. Once stems reach 8-10 inches, snip the green stems from the top. Leave woody sections alone and keep at least 5 inches of plant.

## Pruning

Pick off any leaf that develops brown spots.

## Pests and disease

Watch for spider mites.
