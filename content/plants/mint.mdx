# Mint

_Mentha_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 60–75 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

Frequent harvests extend its life, taking single leaves or cutting stems above the nodes. Though slow to germinate, it grows fast once established. Cut it back to 1/3 of its height each time for repeat harvests.

## Pruning

Pick off brown-spotted leaves and red runners, which pull energy away from leaf growth. It regrows fast, so prune hard when needed.

## Pests and disease

Watch for spider mites.
