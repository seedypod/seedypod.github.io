# Stock Flower

_Matthiola incana_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–10 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 65–80 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 55–75 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

For a vase, cut long stems as the blooms begin to open and put them in water right away. Blooms last longest left on the plant.

## Pruning

Snip off yellow or brown leaves. When the main head starts to fade, remove the whole head at the base of its stalk rather than picking off petals. Smaller side shoots will then flower.

## Pests and disease

Watch for spider mites.

## Climate

Too much heat keeps it from blooming.
