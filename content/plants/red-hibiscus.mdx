# Red Hibiscus

_Hibiscus moscheutos_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–20 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 100–130 days
- **Height:** 2-3 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Petals and young leaves are edible, and the leaves work raw or cooked. For petals, snip each flower at its stem and pull them off, leaving the center. For a vase, cut a flower just before it opens, when the petals are only starting to show, and stand it in water.

## Pruning

Check and trim roots twice a month, since the root system gets large with age. Before flowering, once it has a few sets of leaves, you can top the main stem back to ⅔ of its height for bushier growth, though buds may arrive a few weeks later. Pinch fading flowers off below the head, and once blooming starts, cut it back to size as needed while keeping ⅔ of its height.

## Support

Secure the tall main stem with a plant belt as it grows.

## Pests and disease

Yellow or brown patches between the veins or along leaf edges are common and harmless. Discolored leaves do not stop it from flowering in repeated waves.
