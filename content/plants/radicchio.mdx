# Radicchio

_Cichorium intybus_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–10 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 65–75 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

Pick outer leaves at 4 inches, just above the base, taking no more than 1/3 of the plant if you want regrowth. For one harvest that lets the inner head develop, cut at the base once the head is about 3-4 inches across and deep burgundy. Outer leaves may loosen from a maturing head but are still good to eat.

## Pests and disease

Heading plants are prone to tip burn, though this variety resists it fairly well. If tip burn shows, cool the plant down and refresh the tank. Watch for aphids.

## Climate

A hybrid that copes with warmer conditions.
