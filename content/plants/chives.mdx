# Chives

_Allium schoenoprasum_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Don’t thin. Let every sprout in the pod grow.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 75–85 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once the roots are 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Let leaves reach at least 4 inches, then cut them back to 1-2 inches above the base. The flowers are edible. Keep cut chives in the fridge's crisper drawer, wrapped loosely in a paper towel.

## Pruning

Cut flowering scapes as they appear to delay bolting.

## Pests and disease

Watch for thrips.
