# Purple Basil

_Ocimum basilicum_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–10 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 72 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

Frequent harvests extend the plant's life. Take single leaves, or cut the stem 1/4 to 1/2 inch above the nodes, the tiny leaf pair or knobs that sit just above a stem's two biggest leaves. Those small leaves then grow into the next harvest.

## Pruning

Pick off any leaf that develops brown spots. Cutting stems back above the nodes on a regular basis makes the plant bushier. Pinch out flower buds as soon as they show to hold off bolting.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids. Root rot and downy mildew are common, and inspecting the roots regularly and pulling brown-spotted leaves early keeps them in check.
