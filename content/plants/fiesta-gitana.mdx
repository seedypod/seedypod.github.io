# Fiesta Gitana

_Calendula officinalis_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 14–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 50–60 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

For a vase, cut long stems when blooms are half open and put them in water right away. For petals, cut off the heads and dry the petals completely, then use them as a garnish or in place of saffron. For medicinal use, take petals before they start to wither on the plant.

## Pruning

Pinch the tips of growing stalks now and then to keep it compact and under the light. Deadhead spent blooms, and cut the leftover bare stem back too so it does not rot.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids and whiteflies.
