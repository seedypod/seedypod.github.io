# Dianthus

_Dianthus chinensis x barbatus_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–7 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 50 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

For a vase, cut stems at a 45° angle just above a leaf node, at whatever height you want.

## Pruning

Deadhead spent blooms to push new buds, and snip off yellow or brown leaves. Always keep at least a third of the plant so it can rebloom.
