# Catnip

_Nepeta cataria_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–14 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 40–50 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Frequent harvests extend the plant's life. Take single leaves, or cut the stem 1/4 to 1/2 inch above the nodes, the tiny leaf pair or knobs that sit just above a stem's two biggest leaves. Those small leaves then grow into the next harvest.

## Pruning

Pick off any leaf that develops brown spots.
