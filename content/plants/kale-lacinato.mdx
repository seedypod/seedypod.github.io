# Kale Lacinato

_Brassica oleracea 'Lacinato'_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 65 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Pick outer leaves at 2-3 inches, cutting just above the base, so the center keeps producing. Keep at least 1/3 of the plant in place if you want it to regrow. For a single harvest, let the plant mature and cut it at the base.
