# Chervil

_Anthriscus cerefolium_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 60–70 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 55–65 °F
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

Flavor peaks while the plant is young and not yet flowering, and frequent harvests extend its life. Cut whole outer branches at the base, always leaving 1/3 of the plant for continued bushy growth. Replace the plant once its leaves go brown or purple.

## Pruning

Pick off brown-spotted leaves and snip flowers as soon as they appear to keep the plant going longer. Its long taproot needs a root check at least monthly.

## Pests and disease

Watch for thrips.

## Climate

Bolts quickly in warm rooms.
