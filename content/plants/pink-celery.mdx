# Pink Celery

_Apium graveolens_ · Vegetable

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 12–20 days
- **First harvest:** 2 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 75–100 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–75 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

Cut stalks at the base once they are about 6 inches or taller; longer growth gives thicker stalks. For ongoing harvests, take the larger outer stalks or pick leaves early, always keeping 1/3 of the plant. For a single harvest, let the plant mature and cut it at the base.

## Pests and disease

Start plant food as early as 10 days after germination is first seen. Germination and early growth are slow, but an established plant is a heavy producer.
