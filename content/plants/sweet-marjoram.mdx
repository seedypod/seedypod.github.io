# Sweet Marjoram

_Origanum syriacum_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 65–75 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Frequent harvests extend its life. Only cut stems once they are at least 4-6 inches tall, taking single leaves or cutting above the nodes. Leave 1/3 of the plant for continued growth.

## Pruning

Pick off any leaf that develops brown spots. Snip flowers as they appear to extend its life.
