# Wasabi Greens

_Brassica juncea_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 4–21 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 45 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner

## Harvest

Pick outer leaves at 4 inches just above the base, keeping at least 1/3 of the plant for regrowth. The whole leaf is edible, stalk included. For a single harvest, let the head mature and cut it at the base; the flowers work as an edible garnish.

## Climate

Heat makes it bolt and turn bitter.
