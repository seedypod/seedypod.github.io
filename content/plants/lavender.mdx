# Lavender

_Lavandula angustifolia_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 15–30 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 100–110 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

With clean shears, cut stems once about half the flowers on them have opened. The stems turn woody and can be hard to cut.

## Pruning

When it first reaches 6-8 inches, cut back 1/3 of the plant so it bushes out instead of growing tall. After that, let it grow and bloom freely, pruning only once a year.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids and whiteflies.
