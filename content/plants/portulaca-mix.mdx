# Portulaca Mix

Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 60–75 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–80 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

Start harvesting once it reaches 10 inches tall. Pinch off single leaves or flowers, or cut stems above a growth node with clean shears. For a vase, cut stems at a 45° angle just above a leaf node, at whatever height you want.

## Pruning

Deadhead spent blooms to push new buds, and snip off yellow or brown leaves. Always keep at least a third of the plant so it can rebloom.

## Pests and disease

It does poorly when kept too wet. Use a lighter watering schedule.

## Climate

Tolerates temperatures down to 60°F.
