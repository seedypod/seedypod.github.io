# Holy Basil

_Ocimum tenuiflorum_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 70 days
- **Height:** under 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–75 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

Frequent harvests extend its life. Take single leaves, or cut the stem 1/4 to 1/2 inch above the nodes, the tiny leaf pair or knobs that sit just above a stem's two biggest leaves. Every part is edible, including stems, leaves, flowers and seeds.

## Pruning

Pick off leaves with brown or yellow spots. Cutting stems above the nodes on a regular basis makes it bushier. Pruning off the edible flowers also encourages bushiness and holds off bolting.

## Pests and disease

Root rot and downy mildew are common. Checking roots regularly and removing brown-spotted leaves early keeps them in check.
