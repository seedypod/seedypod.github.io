# Zinnia

_Zinnia elegans_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 60–75 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner

## Harvest

For petals, snip each flower at its stem and pull the petals, avoiding the bitter center. For a vase, cut just before the bloom opens, when the petals are nearly unfurled, and stand it in water.

## Pruning

Snip off yellow or brown leaves, and deadhead spent blooms to push new buds.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.

## Climate

Tolerates temperatures down to 60°F. Keep humidity moderate, since too much makes the plant unhealthy.
