# Bull's Blood Beets

_Beta vulgaris_ · Vegetable

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–10 days
- **First harvest:** 2 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 35 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner

## Harvest

Pick outer leaves at 4 inches just above the base and keep at least 1/3 of the plant for regrowth. For one harvest, take every leaf together with the small beetroot.
