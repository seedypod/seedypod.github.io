# Shiso

_Perilla frutescens var. crispa_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 50–60 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

Frequent harvests extend its life. Take leaves one at a time or by the stem. Harvesting can start while the plant is young, as long as 1/3 of the leaves stay on.

## Pruning

Pick off brown-spotted leaves. For bushier rather than taller growth, top each stem by cutting its main growing tip at the height you want. Remove flower buds as they appear to delay bolting and keep leaves coming.
