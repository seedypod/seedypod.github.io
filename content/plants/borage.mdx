# Borage

_Borago officinalis_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–10 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 50–60 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner

## Harvest

Blooms appear 6-8 weeks after sprouting. Snip or pinch open flowers at their base. Take leaves by cutting where they join the main stem.

## Pruning

Remove older leaves as they yellow or brown, cutting where they join the main stem.

## Support

If it gets top-heavy, steady it with a plant belt.
