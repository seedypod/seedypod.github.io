# Hungarian Hot Wax Pepper

_Capsicum annuum_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–28 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 60–90 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Pick once the smooth, waxy peppers reach at least 5 inches long and 1 inch wide. Color moves from yellow to orange and finally red as they ripen. Cut each pepper off at its own stem.

## Pruning

Regular pruning is needed. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown. Trim back branches that grow out of the light.

## Support

Tie the plant up as it matures, since it can get quite tall and carries heavy fruit.

## Pollinating

Flowers show up 4-6 weeks after sprouting. Pinch off that first round of buds so the plant puts energy into roots and leaves. Pollinate the next flowers by shaking the whole plant to mimic wind, or brush inside each bloom with a fingertip or small brush.

## Pests and disease

Mild edema is a harmless disorder and won't stop this plant from producing heavily. Watch for aphids.
