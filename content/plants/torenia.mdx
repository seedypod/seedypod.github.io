# Torenia

_Torenia fournieri_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 6–20 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 60–75 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

For garnish, pinch single blooms off just behind the head. For a vase, cut stems just above the last set of healthy leaves and put them in water.

## Pruning

Pinch the tips of growing stalks now and then for bushier growth. Deadhead spent blooms at their base, and cut that stem back to just above the first set of full, healthy leaves to keep it tidy and prevent rot.

## Pests and disease

Watch for spider mites.
