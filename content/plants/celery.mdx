# Celery

_Apium graveolens_ · Vegetable

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 14–30 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 75–100 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–75 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Let stalks reach about 6 inches before cutting them at the base. Leaves can be picked sooner, but leave ⅓ of the plant so the stalks keep developing.
