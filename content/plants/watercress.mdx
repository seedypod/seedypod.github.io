# Watercress

_Nasturtium officinale_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 60 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Frequent picking extends the plant's life. Pinch single leaves, or cut stems just above a growth node with clean shears. Keep ⅓ of the plant in place for later harvests.

## Pruning

Pull off leaves that develop brown spots. If growth turns spindly, cut every branch back by at least ⅓ to make it bushier.

## Pests and disease

Watch for spider mites.
