# Garlic Chives

_Allium tuberosum_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Don’t thin. Let every sprout in the pod grow.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 90 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once the roots are 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

Let leaves reach at least 4-6 inches before cutting them back to 1-2 inches above the base, and they regrow in 1-2 weeks. Pick flower buds as soon as they form to lengthen the plant's life, and use them as a garnish.

## Climate

Slow to germinate, usually 21-28 days to first sprout. Sprouting goes best around 65-70°F.
