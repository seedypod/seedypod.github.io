# Cucumber

_Cucumis sativus_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 65–75 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Pick when fruit is 3-6 inches long and dark green, and the yellow flower has dropped from the tip. Cut each cucumber off at its own stem. Fruit left too long turns bitter.

## Pruning

Pinch off the 5 lowest flowers. After that, remove extra flowers so no more than 5 cucumbers grow on the plant at once. An overloaded plant lets young fruit yellow or brown and shrivel before maturing. Top the main stem at your chosen height to push side branches, which keeps the plant bushy and inside the light.

## Support

Train the vine up the tower columns with a trellis and plant ties to hold the fruit.

## Pollinating

No pollination is needed. This variety grows only female flowers.

## Pests and disease

Watch for thrips.

## Climate

Cucumbers drink heavily and do best in fresh water. Keep the tank above 60% and top it off every week.
