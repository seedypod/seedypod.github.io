# Hon Tsai Tai

_Brassica rapa_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–10 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 40–50 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

Pick outer leaves at 4 inches just above the base for sweet, tender greens, keeping at least 1/3 of the plant to regrow. Take the thin red-purple flower stems at 8-10 inches long, just as the blooms start to open. Ripe stems snap off cleanly; ones that resist are overmature and can be fibrous.

## Pruning

Its roots grow vigorously. Regular harvesting means it needs little pruning beyond trimming yellow or brown leaves.
