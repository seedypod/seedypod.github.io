# Fairytale Eggplant

_Solanum melongena_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–21 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 65–85 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Pick once fruit is 2-4 inches long and about 1 inch across, singly or as a cluster. Cut at each stem with clean shears. Glossy skin means ready, and dull skin means overripe. Picking often keeps new fruit coming.

## Pruning

Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown. Trim back branches that grow out of the light.

## Support

Tie the plant up as it matures so the fruit load doesn't pull it over.

## Pollinating

Hand-pollination is needed. Once purple flowers open, shake the whole plant to mimic wind, or brush inside each bloom with a fingertip or small brush.

## Pests and disease

Mild edema is a harmless disorder and won't stop this plant from producing heavily. Watch for aphids.
