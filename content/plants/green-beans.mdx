# Green Beans

_Phaseolus vulgaris_ · Legume

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 50–75 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once the roots are 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert
- **Yield:** Light

## Harvest

Pick round, fleshy pods about 5 inches long and roughly pencil-thick, while still tender and before full maturity, usually 1-2 weeks after flowering. Snip each pod at its stem. This bush bean sets most of its crop at once, but regular picking keeps it producing.

## Pruning

Little pruning is needed. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown.

## Support

The plant stays compact, but a trellis and plant ties up the tower columns help carry the pods.

## Pollinating

White, pink, and purple flowers appear 4-6 weeks after sprouting. Help them set pods by shaking the plant gently or brushing inside each bloom with a fingertip or small brush.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids and mites.
