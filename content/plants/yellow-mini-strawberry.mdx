# Yellow Mini Strawberry

_Fragaria vesca_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–15 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 100–120 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 65–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

These berries never turn red; they go from white-yellow to creamy yellow as they ripen. Ripeness shows in color, not size, so wait until the berry is creamy yellow right up to the stem. Leave ripe berries a day or two longer for the best flavor, until they feel soft rather than firm. Pinch them off or snip each stem with clean scissors.

## Pruning

Remove no more than ⅓ of the leaves at one time, and cut off any yellow or brown ones. Cut away every runner for the plant's whole life. Trim roots twice a month, removing any that are brown, black, slimy, or past the yPod.

## Pollinating

Pollinate each white flower by hand once it opens. Cradle it between your index and middle fingers and rub your thumb around the whole flower head 2-3 times, covering every part. Patchy pollination gives oddly shaped but still edible berries. A pollinated flower sheds its petals and swells into a berry.

## Pests and disease

The roots are sensitive and prone to root rot. Use the bloom-and-fruit nutrient dose of 1 teaspoon per gallon of tank water. Watch for aphids and spider mites. Seeds can be slow to sprout.

## Climate

Keep relative humidity at 60-75%. Brown leaf edges mean the air is too dry, so set a small humidifier near the tower.
