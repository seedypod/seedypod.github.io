# Mini Okra

_Abelmoschus esculentus_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–16 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 60–75 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Pick pods at 3-4 inches long to keep the plant producing, clipping each at its base. Pods left too long grow oversized and tough. Opening flowers are edible too, but a picked flower never becomes a pod.

## Pruning

Regular pruning is needed. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown.

## Support

Tie the stem up as the plant gets tall.

## Pests and disease

Small clear beads on stems and leaves that look like jelly or water drops are natural sap. This is normal.

## Climate

Okra wants full light. Because it grows tall, place it so most of the plant sits in the high light zone, even if its base ends up in a medium or low light spot.
