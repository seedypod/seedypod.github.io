# Sage

_Salvia officinalis_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 80–90 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Take single leaves, or cut stems back to 4-6 inches tall. To dry, hang whole stems for at least two weeks, then store in an airtight container.

## Pruning

Pick off brown-spotted leaves. It grows slowly but tall, so trim stem tops to push it toward a bushier shape.

## Pests and disease

Prone to a wide range of common pests.
