# Mini Cauliflower

_Brassica oleracea botrytis_ · Vegetable

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–10 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 50–60 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 65–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Light

## Harvest

Cut the head at its base once it is 2-4 inches wide. It will not grow a second head. The leaves are edible raw or cooked, so take them at the same time.

## Pruning

Needs little pruning beyond trimming yellow or brown leaves when they appear.
