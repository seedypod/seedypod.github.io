# Lemon Hot Pepper

_Capsicum annuum_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–21 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 100–115 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Peppers grow to about 1 inch long and ½ inch wide and ripen from green to lemon-yellow. Pick once yellow for the most heat. Cut each pepper off at its own stem.

## Pruning

Little pruning is needed. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown.

## Support

Tie the plant up as it matures so the fruit load doesn't pull it over.

## Pollinating

Flowers show up 4-6 weeks after sprouting. Pinch that first round of buds off from behind so the plant puts energy into roots and leaves. Pollinate the next flowers by shaking the whole plant to mimic wind, or brush inside each bloom with a fingertip or small brush.

## Pests and disease

Mild edema is a harmless disorder and won't stop this plant from producing heavily. Watch for aphids.
