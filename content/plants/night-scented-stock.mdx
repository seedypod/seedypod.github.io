# Night-Scented Stock

_Matthiola longipetala_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–20 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 50–70 days
- **Height:** under 2 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

For a vase, wait until the flowers are nearly open, cut low on the stalk, and put it in water right away. Single blooms can be pinched off where they join the stem; use them right away.

## Pruning

Deadhead spent blooms, and cut the leftover bare stem back too so it does not rot.
