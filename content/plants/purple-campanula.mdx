# Purple Campanula

_Campanula medium_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 12–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 70–80 days
- **Height:** 2.5-3 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

For a vase, cut a stem once 2-3 of its lower buds have opened. Put it straight into water so the remaining buds open slowly.

## Pruning

Remove leaves that develop brown spots. Deadhead spent blooms, and cut the leftover bare stem back so energy goes to new buds.

## Support

It trails down the tower on its own. Use a plant belt if you would rather train it upward.

## Pests and disease

Watch for spider mites.
