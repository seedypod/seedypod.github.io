# Bare Root Strawberries

_Fragaria ananassa_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Don’t thin. Let every sprout in the pod grow.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–24 days (typical for its kind)
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 60–90 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Ripeness shows in color, not size, so wait until the berry is red right up to the stem. Any white or pale red areas mean it needs more time. Leave ripe berries a day or two longer for the best flavor, until they feel soft rather than firm. Pinch them off or snip each stem with clean scissors.

## Pruning

Remove no more than ⅓ of the leaves at one time, and cut off any yellow or brown ones. Snip off flowers until the plant has at least 6 fully open leaves. Cut away every runner for the plant's whole life. Trim roots twice a month, removing any that are brown, black, slimy, or past the yPod.

## Pollinating

Pollinate each white flower by hand once it opens. Cradle it between your index and middle fingers and rub your thumb around the whole flower head 2-3 times, covering every part. Patchy pollination gives oddly shaped but still edible berries. A pollinated flower sheds its petals and grows into a berry over about 2 weeks.

## Pests and disease

The roots are very sensitive and highly prone to root rot. Watch for aphids and spider mites.

## Climate

Keep humidity at 60-75%. Brown leaf edges mean the air is too dry, so set a humidifier near the tower.
