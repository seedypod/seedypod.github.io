# Tarragon

_Tagetes lucida_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 65–75 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Start at 8 inches tall and harvest often, taking larger leaves or cutting stems above the nodes while leaving 1/3 of the plant. Pinch flowers off at their base to harvest them. To dry, hang bundled cut branches until they snap rather than bend, then strip the leaves and flowers.

## Pruning

Pick off brown-spotted leaves. Pinch the growing tips to make it bushier. Pinch off flowers as they appear, and note they are edible.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.
