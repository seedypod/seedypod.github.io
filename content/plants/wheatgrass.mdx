# Wheatgrass

_Triticum aestivum_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Don’t thin. Let every sprout in the pod grow.
- **Sprouts in:** 1–7 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 21 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once the roots are 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Cut once blades are at least 4 inches tall, gathering a bunch in one hand and slicing through it with clean scissors in the other. To regrow, leave at least 2 inches standing.
