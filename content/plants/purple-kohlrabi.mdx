# Purple Kohlrabi

_Brassica oleracea_ · Vegetable

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–10 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 45–55 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Light

## Harvest

Once the bulb is 3-4 inches across, cut at the stem base to take bulb and leaves in one harvest. Some leaves can be picked earlier, but keep ⅓ of them so the bulb keeps growing.
