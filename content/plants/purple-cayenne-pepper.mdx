# Purple Cayenne Pepper

_Capsicum annuum_ · Fruiting plant

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–28 days
- **First harvest:** 3–4 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 90–100 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 70–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Pick at around 3 inches long. Take them green for a slightly milder flavor, or wait until they turn purple for the most heat. Cut each pepper off at its own stem.

## Pruning

Trim back branches and leaves to keep the plant inside the light. Cut off any leaves that yellow or brown.

## Support

Tie the plant up as it matures so the fruit load doesn't pull it over.

## Pollinating

White and purple flowers show up 4-6 weeks after sprouting. Pinch off that first round of buds so the plant puts energy into roots and leaves. Pollinate later flowers by shaking the whole plant or rubbing inside each bloom with a fingertip or small brush.

## Pests and disease

Mild edema is a harmless disorder and won't stop this plant from producing heavily. Watch for aphids.
