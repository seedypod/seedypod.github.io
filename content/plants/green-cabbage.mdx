# Green Cabbage

_Brassica oleracea_ · Vegetable

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–20 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 80–115 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 55–75 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

For repeat picking, cut outer leaves just above the base and let the center keep growing, keeping at least 1/3 of the plant. For a single harvest, let the head mature and cut it at the base.

## Pruning

Trim off yellow or brown leaves when they show up.

## Support

Needs no support as a rule. A heavy head can make water drip from the yCube; pick more leaves to lighten it or add a plant belt.

## Climate

A closed head forms best at 55-75°F. Warmer conditions give a looser plant that still yields large, thick, crunchy leaves.
