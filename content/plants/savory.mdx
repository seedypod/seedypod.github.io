# Savory

_Satureja hortensis_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–14 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 50–60 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 65–85 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Start harvesting at 6 inches, taking single leaves or cutting above the nodes, and cut often to extend its life. Flavor peaks once flower buds form but before they open. At that point, cut the leafy tops at the base of the stem.

## Pruning

Pick off any leaf that develops brown spots. Snip flowers as they appear to extend its life.

## Pests and disease

Watch for spider mites.
