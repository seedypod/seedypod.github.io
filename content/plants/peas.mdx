# Peas

_Pisum sativum_ · Legume

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 60–80 days
- **Height:** under 2 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Once pods look plump, pick every few days to keep new pods forming. Cut them from the vine and eat right away, or refrigerate and use within a week. Pulling the string off the back of the pod is optional. If the pods turn tough, shell them and eat the peas inside.

## Support

The vine climbs with 1-inch tendrils that grab any thin support within reach. Guide it up the tower columns with a trellis and plant ties to carry the pods.

## Pollinating

Help flowers set pods by shaking the whole plant gently or brushing inside each bloom with a fingertip or small brush.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids and thrips. Pods sometimes grow neoplasm, a harmless bumpy growth.
