# Collard Greens

_Brassica oleracea var. acephala_ · Leafy green

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–21 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 60–70 days
- **Height:** under 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 55–75 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner

## Harvest

For repeat picking, cut lower leaves just above the stem and let the top keep growing, taking no more than 1/3 of the plant at a time. For one harvest, cut the mature plant at the stem base, then strip the leaves off.

## Pruning

Trim off yellow or brown leaves when they show up. Cut yellow flower stems as they form to hold off bolting.

## Pests and disease

A waxy, glossy leaf coating helps fend off pests naturally.

## Climate

Still produces in warmer conditions.
