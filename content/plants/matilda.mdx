# Matilda

_Lactuca sativa_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 52 days
- **Height:** under 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner

## Harvest

Pick outer leaves at 4 inches, cutting just above the base, so the center keeps producing. Keep at least 1/3 of the plant in place if you want it to regrow. For a single harvest, let the head mature and cut it at the base.

## Climate

Heat makes it bolt and turn bitter.
