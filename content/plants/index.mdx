# Plants

Every yCube in seedypod's catalog, 139 in all. Each page says how many sprouts to keep when you thin the pod, how long the plant takes to sprout and to reach its first harvest, and how to harvest, prune and look after it. The facts come from Gardyn's plant pages and yCube store; the [catalog provenance](/knowledge/plants/catalog-provenance/) note says which.

Thin each pod to its count once the seedlings are up, cutting the extras at the base rather than pulling them. [Sprouts to keep](/knowledge/plants/thinning/) explains the counts and where Gardyn contradicts itself.
