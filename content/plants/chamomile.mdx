# Chamomile

_Matricaria recutita_ · Flower

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Don’t thin. Let every sprout in the pod grow.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–14 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 75–120 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate

## Harvest

Pick blooms once fully open by pinching or snipping at the base of the head. Regular picking keeps new flowers coming and extends the plant's life.

## Pruning

Remove any leaves that develop brown spots.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.
