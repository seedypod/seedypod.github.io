# Italian Parsley

_Petroselinum crispum neopolitanum_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 10–30 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 70–90 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Temperature:** 60–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [Medium](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

Frequent harvests extend its life. Once it reaches 6 inches, start taking the larger outer leaves, either singly or by cutting stems above the nodes. Always leave 1/3 of the plant's height so it keeps growing.

## Pruning

Pick off any leaf that develops brown spots.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.
