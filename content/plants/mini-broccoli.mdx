# Mini Broccoli

_Brassica oleracea Italica_ · Vegetable

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 1 sprout per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 5–10 days
- **First harvest:** 2 months after planting (typical for its kind)
- **Matures in:** 85–100 days
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Temperature:** 65–70 °F
- **Light zone:** [High](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Expert

## Harvest

Cut the stem just below the main head once it is 1.5-2 inches across, about large-egg size, before it starts to spread. Side stems follow 15-20 days later; take them at 4-6 inches long and 1-1.5 inches thick, and expect repeat cuts if conditions allow. Leave the edible lower leaves until the stems are finished, then snip them just above the stem.

## Pruning

Needs little pruning beyond trimming yellow or brown leaves when they appear.

## Support

Use a plant belt to support it as it matures.
