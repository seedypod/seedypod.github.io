# Bunching Onions

_Allium fistulosum_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–21 days
- **First harvest:** 2–3 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 60 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once the roots are 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Intermediate
- **Yield:** Generous

## Harvest

Wait until stalks are at least 6-8 inches tall, then cut them back to about 2 inches above the base. The cut stalks are round and hollow and grow back in 1-2 weeks.

## Climate

Slow to germinate. Sprouting goes best around 65-70°F and can take 21 days or more.
