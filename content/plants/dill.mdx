# Dill

_Anethum graveolens_ · Herb

## At a glance

- **Thinning:** Keep 3 sprouts per pod and cut the rest at the base.
- **Sprouts in:** 7–24 days
- **First harvest:** 1–2 months after planting
- **Matures in:** 40–50 days
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Light zone:** [Low](/knowledge/plants/gardyn-light-zones/)
- **Full dose of plant food:** Once it has true leaves or roots 1/2 inch long
- **Care level:** Beginner
- **Yield:** Bountiful

## Harvest

Frequent harvests extend its life. Once mature, cut back the top third every 2 months and take small fronds whenever needed. Harvesting from the top down makes it bushier.

## Pruning

Pick off any leaf that develops brown spots. Pinch off flowers when they show so the plant keeps putting energy into leaves.

## Pests and disease

Watch for aphids.
